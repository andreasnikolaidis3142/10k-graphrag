# What was the effective tax rate, and what drove the difference from the U.S. statutory rate?

## Summary
Walmart's effective income tax rate was 25.5% for fiscal 2024 [F00872], down from 33.6% in fiscal 2023 [F00873] and 25.4% in fiscal 2022 [F00874]. The elevated FY2023 rate was primarily driven by the tax impact of the business reorganization resulting in the full separation of PhonePe from Flipkart [T00460]. Income tax expense for FY2024 was $5.58B [F00042] on pre-tax income of $21.85B [F00039]. The FY2024 rate normalized back to FY2022 levels once the one-time reorganization impact was removed.

**Status:** neutral
