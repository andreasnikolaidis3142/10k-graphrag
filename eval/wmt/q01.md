## Summary
Total revenue grew steadily over the three-year window, rising from $567.76B in FY2022 [F00005] to $605.88B in FY2023 (+6.7% YoY) [F00004] and $642.64B in FY2024 (+6.1% YoY) [F00003], a 6.4% CAGR and +13% over the window. Operating income was volatile: it fell 21.3% YoY to $20.43B in FY2023 [F00019] from $25.94B in FY2022 [F00020], then rebounded 32.2% to $27.01B in FY2024 [F00018], ending the window +4% above the FY2022 level. Operating margin mirrored this trajectory: 4.6% in FY2022 [F01836], down 120 bps to 3.4% in FY2023 [F01837], then up 83 bps to 4.2% in FY2024 [F01838]. FY2024 revenue growth was driven primarily by Walmart US (58% of the total change) and Walmart International (37%), while FY2024 operating income growth was driven by Corporate And Support's $2.88B improvement (44% of the total change) alongside Walmart International (+30%) and Walmart US (+23%). Net income followed a similar pattern to operating income, falling to $11.29B in FY2023 [F00046] before spiking to $16.27B in FY2024 [F00045].
