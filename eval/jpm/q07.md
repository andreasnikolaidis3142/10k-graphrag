## Summary
Noninterest expense for JPMorgan Chase was $87.2 billion in FY2023, up 14% year over year [T00962]. The increase was predominantly driven by higher compensation expense, reflecting growth in employees primarily in technology and front office, as well as wage inflation [T00962]. Share-based compensation specifically rose to $3.12 billion in FY2023, an 11.6% increase from $2.79 billion in FY2022 [F03451]. The noninterest expense increase also includes the $2.9 billion FDIC special assessment and costs associated with the First Republic acquisition [T00962]. The efficiency (overhead) ratio is not in the retrieved evidence.
