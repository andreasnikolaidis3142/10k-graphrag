# How did total net revenue, net income and return on equity change over the last three years?

## Summary
Total net revenue grew steadily over the three-year window, rising from $121.65B in FY2021 [F06823] to $128.69B in FY2022 [F06796] and reaching $158.10B in FY2023 [F06769]. Net income moved in the opposite direction in FY2022, declining from $48.33B in FY2021 [F00080] to $37.68B in FY2022 [F00079], before recovering to $49.55B in FY2023 [F00078], exceeding the FY2021 level. Return on equity followed the net income pattern, falling from 16.4% in FY2021 [F13531] to 12.8% in FY2022 [F13532] (-358 bps) and rebounding to 16.0% in FY2023 [F13533] (+313 bps). The FY2022 compression in net income and ROE coincides with a period of only modest revenue growth. FY2023 delivered the strongest revenue growth and a net income recovery that restored ROE to near FY2021 levels.
