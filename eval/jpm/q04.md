## Summary
JPMorgan Chase reported CET1 capital of $251 billion as of December 31, 2023, with both the Standardized and Advanced CET1 capital ratios at 15.0% [T00978]. The Standardized CET1 capital ratio requirement including regulatory buffers was 11.4% as of December 31, 2023 [T01637], so the actual Standardized CET1 ratio of 15.0% exceeded the requirement. The Advanced CET1 ratio of 15.0% also exceeded the applicable requirement, which comprises a minimum plus a 4.0% GSIB surcharge and a 2.5% capital conservation buffer [T04183]. The countercyclical buffer is currently set to 0% [T04183]. The evidence does not provide the actual Tier 1 or Total capital ratio values for FY2023, only the CET1 ratios [T00978].
