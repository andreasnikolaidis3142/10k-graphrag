# How dependent is Apple on China, and what happened to its sales there?

## Summary
Apple's Greater China region generated $72.56B in FY2023 net sales, representing 18.9% of total company revenue of $383.29B [F00850][F00013]. China was one of only two countries (alongside the U.S.) accounting for more than 10% of net sales across 2021-2023 [T00600], making it a significant but secondary market after the U.S. China sales declined $1.64B (-2.2%) in FY2023 versus $74.20B in FY2022 [F00851][F00850], contributing 15% of the company's total $11.04B (-2.8%) revenue decrease [F00014]. The Americas segment was the largest contributor to the total revenue decline, falling $7.10B (-4.2%) and accounting for 64% of the total change [F00838]. The broader revenue decline was primarily driven by Mac (-$10.82B, 98% of total change) and iPhone (-$4.91B, 44% of total change) [F00282][F00279]. Management attributed the total decline primarily to foreign currency weakness relative to the U.S. dollar [T00275].
