## Summary
Apple's balance sheet is fundamentally strong on net cash but shows liquidity tension. As of FY2023-09-30, the company holds net cash of -$51.01B [F01425], up from -$49.04B in FY2022 [F01424], driven by cash and cash equivalents rising to $29.96B [F00091] and short-term investments to $31.59B [F00093]. Total debt declined 7.5% to $111.09B [F01423], and debt-to-equity improved to 1.79x [F01427] from 2.37x [F01426]. However, the current ratio remains below 1.0 at 0.99x [F01421] versus 0.88x [F01420], and interest coverage fell 29% to 29.06x [F01432]. Management states cash, cash equivalents and unrestricted marketable securities totaled $148.3B as of September 30, 2023, and believes these plus operating cash flow and debt market access will be sufficient [T00350].
