# What did Apple spend on R&D and SG&A, and how did that change relative to revenue?

*model: apodex/apodex-1.1-mini:free | ungrounded numbers: none | missing contributors: none | uncited claims: 0*

## Summary
In FY2023, Apple spent $29.91B on R&D, up 14.0% YoY from $26.25B in FY2022 [F00028][F00029], while SG&A spending was $24.93B, down 0.6% YoY from $25.09B in FY2022 [F00031][F00032]. Revenue fell 2.8% YoY to $383.29B in FY2023 from $394.33B in FY2022 [F00013][F00014]. R&D grew at more than five times the rate of the revenue decline, so R&D intensity rose relative to revenue, whereas SG&A declined slightly less than revenue, also raising SG&A intensity modestly. The revenue decline was driven primarily by Mac, which fell $10.82B (-26.9%), accounting for 98% of the total revenue change [F00282], and by the Americas segment, which fell $7.10B (-4.2%), accounting for 64% of the total change [F00838], partially offset by Services growth of $7.07B (+9.1%) [F00010]. Management attributes the revenue weakness to foreign currency headwinds and lower Mac and iPhone net sales [T00275].
