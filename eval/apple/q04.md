# Are there any red flags in Apple's financial statements?

*model: apodex/apodex-1.1-mini:free | ungrounded numbers: none | missing contributors: none | uncited claims: 0*

## Summary
Apple's FY2023 10-K (fiscal year ended September 30, 2023) carries several pre-computed red flags, though none are severe: the current ratio was below 1.0 in both FY2022 (0.88) and FY2023 (0.99) [F01420][F01421], and FY2023 inventory grew 28% while cost of revenue fell 4% [F00099][F00022]. Operating cash flow declined 9.5% to $110.54B and free cash flow fell 10.6% to $99.58B in FY2023 [F00224][F01389]. Revenue fell 2.8% to $383.29B and operating income fell 4.3% to $114.30B [F00013][F00037], while interest coverage dropped 29% from 40.7x to 29.1x [F01432]. Shareholder payouts (dividends plus buybacks) of $92.58B consumed 93% of free cash flow in FY2023 [F01448][F01389]. The auditor issued an unqualified opinion dated November 2, 2023 [T00627], and all 3 accounting identities passed with no text-vs-table conflicts, so these are watch items rather than confirmed problems.
