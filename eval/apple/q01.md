# How have gross margin and operating margin changed over the last three years, and what drove the change?

*model: apodex/apodex-1.1-mini:free | ungrounded numbers: none | missing contributors: none | uncited claims: 0*

## Summary
Gross margin expanded over the three-year window, rising from 41.8% in FY2021 [F01390] to 43.3% in FY2022 [F01391] (+153 bps) and 44.1% in FY2023 [F01392] (+82 bps), even as gross profit peaked at $170.78B in FY2022 [F00026] and declined 1.0% to $169.15B in FY2023 [F00025]. The FY2023 gross margin gain occurred because cost of revenue fell 4.2% to $214.14B [F00022], outpacing the 2.8% revenue decline to $383.29B [F00013]. Services gross margin increased in 2023 versus 2022, driven by higher Services net sales and partially offset by foreign currency weakness and higher Services costs [T00333]. Operating margin rose from 29.8% in FY2021 [F01393] to 30.3% in FY2022 [F01394] (+51 bps), then fell back to 29.8% in FY2023 [F01395] (-47 bps) as operating income declined 4.3% to $114.30B [F00037] while operating expenses grew 6.8% to $54.85B [F00034].

## Drivers
- FY2022 gross margin expansion (+153 bps) driven by gross profit growing +11.7% [F00026] faster than revenues growing +7.8% [F00014], with cost of revenue rising only +5.0% [F00023].
- FY2023 gross margin expansion (+82 bps) driven by cost of revenue declining -4.2% [F00022] faster than revenues declining -2.8% [F00013].
- FY2023 gross profit decline of -$1.63B was driven by Products segment falling -$5.92B (-5.2%) [F01002], the LARGEST CONTRIBUTOR TO THE CHANGE, partially offset by Services segment rising $4.29B (+7.7%) [F01005].
- FY2023 operating margin contraction (-47 bps) driven by operating expenses growing +6.8% [F00034] while revenue declined, with R&D the LARGEST CONTRIBUTOR TO THE CHANGE at +$3.66B (+14.0%) [F00028], partially offset by SG&A declining -$162.00M (-0.6%) [F00031].
- Services gross margin increase in 2023 attributed to higher Services net sales, partially offset by foreign currency weakness and higher Services costs [T00333].
