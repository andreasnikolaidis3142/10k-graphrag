# How dependent is Apple on China, and what happened to its sales there?

*model: inclusionai/ling-3.1-flash | ungrounded numbers: none | missing contributors: none | uncited claims: 0*

## Summary
Apple's dependence on China is material but secondary to the U.S.: Greater China generated $72.56B, or 18.9% of FY2023 net sales [F00850][F00880], and the U.S. and China were the only countries accounting for more than 10% of net sales in FY2023, FY2022 and FY2021 [T00600]. China sales fell 2.2% year-over-year, from $74.20B in FY2022 [F00851][F00881] to $72.56B in FY2023, a -$1.64B change equal to 15% of the company's total -$11.04B (-2.8%) revenue decline [F00014]. In the business-segment view, Americas was the LARGEST CONTRIBUTOR TO THE CHANGE at -$7.10B (-4.2%), 64% of the total change [F00838][F00839]; in the geographical view the U.S. led with -$9.29B (-6.3%), 84% of the change [F00877]. By product, Mac was the largest contributor (-$10.82B, -26.9%, 98% of the change) [F00282] and iPhone also declined (-$4.91B, -2.4%, 44%) [F00279], while Services grew +$7.07B (+9.1%), offsetting 64% of the decline [F00010]. Management states that foreign currency weakness relative to the U.S. dollar accounted for more than the entire year-over-year decrease in total net sales [T00275].

- Management attributes more than the entire year-over-year decrease in total net sales to foreign currency weakness, with the decline consisting primarily of lower Mac and iPhone sales partially offset by higher Services [T00275].

## Risks and red flags
- Apple has international operations with sales outside the U.S. representing a majority of total net sales, and a majority of its supplier facilities, including manufacturing and assembly sites, are located outside the U.S., so operations depend significantly on global and regional economic conditions [T00118].
- Primary exposure to foreign exchange rate movements relates to non-U.S. dollar-denominated sales, cost of sales and operating expenses worldwide; gross margins on products in foreign countries and on products with foreign-sourced components have been and could be materially adversely affected by FX fluctuations [T00221].
- Greater China's 18.9% revenue share [F00850] leaves a meaningful portion of the top line exposed to China-specific conditions, though the U.S. remains the largest market at 36.2% [F00877].
