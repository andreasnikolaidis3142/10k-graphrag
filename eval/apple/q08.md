# What was Apple's effective tax rate, and why was it below the US statutory rate?

*model: apodex/apodex-1.1-mini:free | ungrounded numbers: none | missing contributors: none | uncited claims: 0*

## Summary
Apple's effective tax rate for FY2023 was 14.7% [F00608] (XBRL-tagged), down from 16.2% in FY2022 [F00609] and 13.3% in FY2021 [F00610]. This rate remained below the 21% statutory federal income tax rate applicable in 2023, 2022, and 2021 [T00519]. Management attributes the discount to the statutory rate primarily to a lower effective tax rate on foreign earnings, the impact of the U.S. federal R&D credit, and tax benefits from share-based compensation, partially offset by state income taxes [T00347]. In FY2023, income tax expense was $16.74B [F00046] on pre-tax income of $113.74B [F00043].

**Status:** positive
