ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Net sales increased 12% to $4.6 billion in fiscal 2023, compared to $4.1 billion in 2022. Gross margin was 43.3%, down 150 basis points from 2022. Net income was $520 million in 2023. We employ approximately 45,000 employees across three reportable segments. We expect net sales of $4.9 to $5.1 billion in 2024. Our 3.5% Senior Notes due 2030 have a face amount of $500 million.
